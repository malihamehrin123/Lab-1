Developer Profile

Hi, I am Maliha Mehrin, a fourth-year Digital Enterprise Management student at the University of Toronto Mississauga. I am interested in business, marketing, and design. Through my studies, I have developed skills in web design, video editing, and digital technology.

Project 1: Formula One Website Design

I designed a Formula One inspired website with a clear, organized, and visually engaging layout. The website included information about the drivers, teams, race schedules, and standings. I wanted the design to reflect the energy of Formula One while still being easy to navigate and use.

Project 2: Monster Energy Drink Commercial

I created and edited a short Monster Energy Drink commercial using Adobe Premiere Pro. I used visual effects, including black-and-white colouring, to create contrast and draw attention to the product. I also worked on the pacing and transitions to make the commercial visually engaging and flow smoothly.

Project 3: Frostbite Quenchers Website Demonstration

I designed a website for a food truck called Frostbite Quenchers and created a video to demonstrate it. The video showed the layout, navigation, and different sections of the website in a clear and organized way. I focused on making the website visually engaging while keeping it simple and easy to navigate.
